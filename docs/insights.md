# E-Commerce Customer Segmentation — Business Insights

1. **4 customer segments identified** using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) and K-Means clustering:
   - **Champions** (Recency ~38 days, Frequency ~5.8, Monetary ~2396) — most recent, frequent, and highest-spending customers.
   - **Loyal/Regular** (Recency ~48 days, Frequency ~3.8, Monetary ~1134) — solid repeat buyers, slightly below Champions.
   - **New/Low-Engagement** (Recency ~57 days, Frequency ~1.6, Monetary ~426) — recent but rarely purchase, low spend.
   - **At-Risk/Lost** (Recency ~259 days, Frequency ~1.4, Monetary ~377) — haven't purchased in ~8-9 months, low historical spend.

2. **Champions and Loyal segments should get retention-focused marketing** (loyalty perks, early access) since they already drive the most revenue.

3. **At-Risk/Lost segment (Recency ~259 days) is the largest re-engagement opportunity** — targeted win-back campaigns (discounts, "we miss you" emails) could recover revenue before these customers churn permanently.

4. **New/Low-Engagement customers need nurturing**, not aggressive selling — since they're recent but haven't built purchase frequency yet, onboarding-style engagement (product recommendations, first-purchase follow-ups) is more appropriate.
